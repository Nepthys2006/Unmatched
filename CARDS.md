# Unmatched: hero and card compendium

Every hero's **identity card** (health, move, attack type, sidekicks, special ability) and **deck** (attack, defense and boost values, and the immediately / during / after combat effects), taken from `assets/data/heroes.json` (corrected against the printed cards, see `tool/card_fixes.json`).

**70 heroes, 880 different cards, 2094 cards counting copies.** Scripted in the engine: 70 of 70.

Generated by `python tool/make_card_compendium.py`; do not edit by hand.

## How to read it

- **Identity card**: the hero and sidekicks with their attack type (melee: adjacent only; ranged: adjacent or anywhere in a shared zone), starting health and how many there are; the move value is shared by all of the hero's fighters.
- **In this game**: how the engine plays the special ability (from the hero script).
- **ATK**: value when played as an attack (attack and versatile cards); the damage before the defense is subtracted.
- **DEF**: value when played as a defense (defense and versatile cards).
- **BOOST**: value added when the card is discarded to boost movement or a combat card. "—" means it cannot boost; "? (die)" is a die roll.
- **Effect order in combat**: IMMEDIATELY → DURING COMBAT → damage → AFTER COMBAT. The defender's effect resolves first at each step.
- Schemes have no ATK/DEF; their text is under *Effect*.
